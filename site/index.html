Adaptive Learning-Path Planner

Given a target concept, a daily time budget and a deadline, the engine finds out what the learner already knows, keeps a per-concept learner state, and builds a day-by-day study plan that never schedules a concept before its prerequisites and never goes over the daily budget. When something changes, it replans.

Made by: Bhuvan and Abdur

What the engine does

Diagnostic

A short adaptive test (IRT computerized adaptive testing) estimates the learner's ability and gives each concept a starting belief.

Code: src/diagnostic/diagnostic.py

Learner state

Each concept keeps a belief that it is known, updated after every answer (Bayesian Knowledge Tracing), and is classed Mastered, Developing, Uncertain or Weak.

Code: src/state/learner_state.py, src/state/policy.py

Daily plan

Concepts are placed day by day in prerequisite order inside the daily budget. A request that cannot fit is rejected with a reason code and a suggested fix. Each decision is written to a trace.

Code: src/planning/service.py, src/planning/static_baseline.py, src/planning/feasibility.py

Replan

On a new assessment result, a missed day, or a changed budget or deadline, the remaining days are planned again; repeated failures trigger remediation of prerequisites.

Code: src/planning/replan.py

Week 7 changes

Adaptive deadlock fix. A concept studied once stayed Uncertain and blocked everything after it, but was never practised again, so adaptive plans stopped after day 1. It now gets another practice session, and its dependents follow.
