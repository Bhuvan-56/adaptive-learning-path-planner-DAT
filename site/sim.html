Live simulation

A simulated learner answers the questions on its own, and the engine diagnoses, plans and replans after every answer. Watch how each answer moves the mastery estimates and the plan. The learner follows the evaluation's S0 simulator; its true knowledge (read only by the evaluator) is shown beside the engine's estimate.

What you are watching

  • Diagnostic first. The CAT asks a few questions and sets a starting estimate for every concept; questions it did not ask are filled in from the ability estimate.
  • Then daily practice. Each day the planner picks concepts whose prerequisites are ready and that fit the daily budget. After every answer the estimate (p) updates and the rest of the plan is rebuilt.
  • Estimate vs truth. The engine never sees the learner's true knowledge; the evaluator does. Differences between the two columns are the engine's mistakes, for example a concept marked Weak after a few unlucky slips.
  • Missed days. In the “skips 4 days” run the learner misses days; the plan is rebuilt with the days that are left.

Why the target ends Uncertain. The target is the last concept in the prerequisite chain, so the plan reaches it only at the end of the horizon and it gets 0 or 1 answers. The engine calls a concept Mastered or Weak only after at least 3 answers and a full window of 4 recent ones, so Uncertain here means “not enough evidence yet”, not “failed”.
