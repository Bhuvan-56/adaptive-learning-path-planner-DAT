One day's study items, each with a duration, a score and prerequisites. Three policies choose items that fit the budget, with every item's prerequisites chosen too: run_greedy_scheduler (score per minute), run_greedy_best_singleton (Week 7 fallback) and run_exhaustive_oracle (exact optimum).
