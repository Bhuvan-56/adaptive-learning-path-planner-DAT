Results from a simulator with synthetic learners (MatchedBKTGenerator), scenarios S0 and S1.

Week 7 fix: the adaptive planner no longer stalls

Before the fix, a concept studied once stayed Uncertain and blocked everything after it, but was never practised again, so adaptive plans stopped after day 1. Now such a concept gets another practice session (PRACTICE_FOR_EVIDENCE) and its dependents follow. Same 1,000 learners before and after.
