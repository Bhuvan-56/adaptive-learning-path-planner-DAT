# Exact Oracle Scaling Measurement

**Algorithm:** exhaustive search over precedence-closed subsets (`src/planning/oracle.py`).
**Limits:** hard cap of 20 activities (raises `ValueError` above that); 2-second per-instance timeout (`timeout_seconds=2.0`).
**Setup:** random DAG instances with exactly N activities, budget fixed at 15 minutes, seed 7, 10 instances per size.

| N | Mean oracle (ms) | Max oracle (ms) | Mean greedy (ms) | Instances |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0.157 | 0.316 | 0.070 | 10 |
| 12 | 0.383 | 0.825 | 0.108 | 10 |
| 16 | 0.579 | 0.891 | 0.141 | 10 |
| 20 | 2.814 | 8.523 | 0.236 | 10 |

**Notes**
- Runtimes are machine-dependent and apply only to small generated instances of up to 20 activities. No claim is made beyond 20 activities.
- An earlier version of this file reported about 0.12 ms for every N. It was generated by slicing 4–12 node benchmark instances, so the larger sizes were never real. It has been replaced by this measurement.
