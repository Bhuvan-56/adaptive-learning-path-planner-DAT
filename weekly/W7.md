## Correction to the Week 6 record

`docs/beta/beta_report.md` Decision D2 calls S1 and S2 "verified sensitivity variants". That wording is not supported. In S1 the integrated loop never applies daily forgetting (defect above), so S1 did not test forgetting during the horizon. The Week 6 tables show S1 and S2 with mastered figures identical to S0. Whether S2's difficulty sensitivity reaches the outcome was not checked this week. S1 and S2 should be described as configured variants whose sensitivity is not verified. They were excluded from the Week 7 comparison by pre-registration.

## Decisions

Decision: Keep the best-singleton fallback as an opt-in policy (`run_greedy_best_singleton` and the arm `non_adaptive_greedy_best_singleton`). `run_greedy_scheduler` and the existing `non_adaptive_greedy` arm stay the default and stay unchanged. Reason: the scheduling gain passes the claim rule and no instance got worse. The integrated gain passes only when the five seeds are pooled; every single seed shows "no difference shown", and the result covers synthetic learners under S0 only. Making the fallback the default needs a new pre-registered run after the S1 fix. Next work, in order: the S1 fix, then the D1 no-plan fix.

Decision (deadlock fix, phase 9): Keep the fix as the default behaviour of `replan`; D1 is reversed. Reason: pre-registered, all hard constraints hold (0 prerequisite and 0 budget violations, 100% trace coverage), adaptive_full improves on every learner it changes (+0.2420 [0.2110, 0.2740], 204 up, 0 down) and is now better than the static order (+0.1400 [0.1110, 0.1690]), and the arms that do not replan are unchanged. With D1 fixed, the next work is the S1 fix, then the two open items above (the planner idles once the deadline cannot be met; ablations that never change a schedule), then the larger curriculum for the final demo.
