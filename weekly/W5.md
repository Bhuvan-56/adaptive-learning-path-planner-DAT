## 3. What Did Not Hold

*The following analysis is copied in substance directly from [evaluation/results/w5/final/README.md](file:///c:/Users/5520/Documents/IITH%20BTECH%20ENGINEERING/SEM%207/distributued%20Ai/Project/adaptive-learning-path-planner-DAT/evaluation/results/w5/final/README.md):*

1. **Adaptive Superiority Did Not Hold:**
   - In both primary comparisons (`adaptive_full` vs `static_prerequisite_order` and `adaptive_full` vs `non_adaptive_greedy`), the paired 95% bootstrap confidence interval was $[-0.1200, -0.0450]$.
   - The adaptive arm **did not beat** either baseline. In fact, both static prerequisite ordering and non-adaptive greedy frontier packing achieved significantly higher mastery ($+0.0800$ concepts, CI $[0.0450, 0.1200]$) than `adaptive_full`.
   - **Root Cause:** In the simulation, dynamic replanning encountered `SCHEDULING_CONSTRAINTS_UNMET` on 800 replan instances (days 2–5) when attempting to re-optimize remaining nodes within the tightly constrained 10-minute daily budget and remaining horizon. This triggered fallback candidate lists, whereas the static schedule reliably packed and executed content without replanning failure.

2. **Baseline Differentiation Did Not Hold:**
   - The comparison `non_adaptive_greedy - static_prerequisite_order` yielded a mean difference of $0.0000$ and a 95% bootstrap CI of $[0.0000, 0.0000]$.
   - The interval includes zero (collapsing to exactly 0), meaning no difference was demonstrated between static topological order and greedy frontier packing on this curriculum and budget.

3. **Ablation Sensitivity Did Not Hold for 4 out of 5 Components:**
   - `ablation_no_diagnostic`: Difference $0.0000$ [0.0000, 0.0000] (no difference shown).
   - `ablation_no_difficulty_step`: Difference $0.0000$ [0.0000, 0.0000] (no difference shown).
   - `ablation_no_review`: Difference $0.0000$ [0.0000, 0.0000] (no difference shown).
   - `ablation_no_remediation`: Difference $0.0000$ [0.0000, 0.0000] (no difference shown).
   - **Root Cause:** Under the default simulator (`MatchedBKTGenerator`), difficulty stepping and spaced review do not affect latent mastery transitions because the default generator has zero forgetting ($p_F = 0$) and does not incorporate item difficulty.
   - Only `ablation_no_replan` differed from `adaptive_full` (Mean Diff: $-0.0800$ $[-0.1200, -0.0450]$), exactly matching the static baseline performance.

## 4. What It Does NOT Show

To ensure rigorous transparency, the team explicitly documents the architectural, empirical, and modeling boundaries of the evaluation:

1. **Synthetic Learners Only:** All evaluated learners and response sequences were generated synthetically using `MatchedBKTGenerator` parameterized by synthetic priors ($p_L=0.10, p_T=0.10, p_S=0.10, p_G=0.25$). Models and parameters are **fitted to synthetic data**, never calibrated against live human learners.
2. **Generators' Assumptions and PROPOSED Parameters:** The generative simulator assumes stationary BKT cognitive transitions without forgetting ($p_F = 0$) in Scenario S0. The parameters proposed for delayed retention quizzes ($N = 5$ items, $D = 7$ days delay, default difficulty $d_0 = 0.50$) represent PROPOSED architectural defaults in `experiments/w5_final.yaml` and `src/evaluation/retention.py`, not empirical human memory parameters.
3. **Retained Recall Measurement ($RR_D$) Status:** Retained recall was **not measured** in the integrated evaluation run. While Task B2 implemented simulator variants (`advance_day` with $p_F > 0$) and the standalone retention evaluator in `src/evaluation/retention.py` (verified via unit tests in `tests/test_w5_b2_simulator_variants.py`), the closed-loop evaluation harness `src/evaluation/integrated.py` only ran Scenario S0 (`MatchedBKTGenerator`). Scenarios S1 (forgetting), S2 (difficulty-sensitive), and S3 (mismatched PFA/logistic) were not executed during `evaluate`.
4. **CAT Stopping Rule Did Not Fire:** The computerized adaptive testing diagnostic specifies a stopping rule of $\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}) \le 0.15$ or a 10-item cap. In simulation, the posterior variances at item 10 were $0.2182$ and $0.2555$, both exceeding $0.15$. Consequently, the early stopping threshold never fired, and all simulated learners took the full 10-item test.
5. **Diagnostic Leaves Every State Uncertain by Design (ADR-009):** The CAT diagnostic estimates latent ability $\hat{\theta}$ and initializes prior mastery probabilities via the logistic link $p_{c,0} = \operatorname{sigm}(\hat{\theta})$. However, it does not inject observation history ($n = 0$). Because ADR-007 and M1-c require $n \ge \text{min\_obs} = 3$ to transition a concept out of `Uncertain`, every concept remains classified as `Uncertain` immediately post-diagnostic by design, until instructional activities are scheduled and observed.

## 6. Scope Accounting: What Was NOT Done & Cut Rules Applied

1. **Integrated Closed-Loop Multi-Scenario Driver (Scenarios S1, S2, S3):**
   - **What Was Not Done:** Automated loop executing scenarios S1, S2, and S3 inside `run_integrated_evaluation`.
   - **Governing Cut Rule:** *Cut Rule 2 (Simulated Precedence & Single-Harness Scoping).* B2 delivered the simulator variants and retention evaluation functions with comprehensive unit tests (`tests/test_w5_b2_simulator_variants.py`). However, B1 scoped the integrated closed-loop harness strictly to the baseline generator (`MatchedBKTGenerator`). Running multi-scenario sweeps within `evaluate` was deferred to prevent combinatorial runtime inflation and preserve clean evaluation comparability.
2. **Automated Combinatorial Repair for Heuristic Infeasibility (Milestone M4-a):**
   - **What Was Not Done:** Algorithmic schedule repack synthesis for NP-hard knapsack packing dead-ends.
   - **Governing Cut Rule:** *Cut Rule 3 (Actionable Bounds over Heuristic Synthesis).* Exact necessary-and-sufficient repairs are generated for single-block exceedances ($> B$) and horizon capacity exceedances ($> DB$). General repack synthesis was bounded to returning `HEURISTIC_NO_PLAN` with structured diagnostics.
3. **Multi-Target Simultaneous Scheduling:**
   - **What Was Not Done:** Concurrent topological path generation for multiple disjoint target competencies.
   - **Governing Cut Rule:** *Cut Rule 4 (Single-Target Coherence).* The engine explicitly bounds planning requests to a single target competency; requests with multiple targets are rejected with `MULTIPLE_TARGETS_UNSUPPORTED`.
4. **Live Human Dataset Training Pipeline (ASSISTments / Junyi):**
   - **What Was Not Done:** Automated ingestion and parameter fitting from production student interaction logs.
   - **Governing Cut Rule:** *Cut Rule 1 (Synthetic-First Simulator Architecture).* As ratified in `decisions/METHODOLOGY_RECORD.md`, the engine operates strictly under synthetic student models fitted to synthetic data.
